# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-16

This Privacy Policy explains how HIX Protocol ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our website, applications, and related services (the "Services").

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on feature usage, we may collect:

- Wallet addresses and on-chain identifiers.
- Device/browser metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamps, diagnostics).
- Usage analytics (page views, feature interactions, error events).
- User-submitted data through forms or app features.
- Data needed to operate leaderboards, rewards, fraud prevention, and support.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Operate and maintain the Services.
- Display rankings and protocol metrics.
- Improve product quality and reliability.
- Detect abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Bases (where applicable)

Where required, we rely on one or more legal bases, such as consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, and legal compliance.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

- Infrastructure/service providers (hosting, analytics, database providers).
- Security and fraud-prevention vendors.
- Regulators, courts, or law enforcement when legally required.
- Successors in a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset transfer.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money unless explicitly stated.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes above, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and enforcement.

## 6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries different from your own. We take steps to protect transferred data as required by law.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

## 9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under the legal age threshold in your jurisdiction. Do not use the Services if prohibited by local law.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies/local storage for core functionality, session state, and analytics. You can manage browser settings, though disabling storage may impact functionality.

## 11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Updated versions will include a new effective date.
